# Maneuver — Company Knowledge Base

## About
Maneuver brings enterprise AI thinking to the businesses that actually run the economy.

We exist to close the gap between enterprise AI consulting and SMBs. 
The same strategic thinking that drives outcomes inside Fortune 500 companies 
shouldn't require a Fortune 500 budget.

## What We Do
We help SMB founders implement enterprise-grade AI systems without enterprise budgets.

AI is an operating shift, not a feature. Every recommendation we make is tied 
to a business metric you actually care about. Every system we build is designed 
to be owned by your team — not to create dependency on ours.

## Services
1. Agentic AI Systems — autonomous agents that handle real business workflows
2. Multimodal AI Platforms — systems that work across text, voice, vision, and data
3. AI Evaluation Frameworks — testing and validation for production AI
4. Enterprise-Grade AI Deployment — scalable, reliable AI infrastructure
5. Voice AI Solutions — conversational AI for customer service, sales, and operations
6. AI Strategy & Implementation — from concept to production, tied to business metrics

## Process
1. Discovery call (30 min) — understand your business metrics and AI opportunity
2. Technical audit (1 week) — review current systems, data, and readiness
3. Implementation sprint (2-4 weeks) — build and deploy AI solution
4. Handoff & optimization (ongoing) — your team owns it, we support as needed
